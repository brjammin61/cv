## Pricing Psychology

### Why These Price Points Work:

**$99/month (BASIC):**
- Low enough for hobbyists to justify
- High enough to filter out tire-kickers
- Annual option: $999/year (2 months free)

**$499/month (PRO):**
- Professional price point
- Easily justifiable if you optimize MEV by even 10%
- Annual option: $4,999/year (2 months free)

**$2,499/month (ENTERPRISE):**
- Institutional budget
- Signals serious, high-value product
- Typically negotiated down to $2k/month (win for customer, still good for you)
- Annual option: $24,999/year ($5k discount)

### Upsell Path:

```
Free User → BASIC ($99) → PRO ($499) → ENTERPRISE ($2,499)
```

**Conversion Rates:**
- Free → Basic: 1% (100/10,000 users)
- Basic → Pro: 20% (20/100 customers)
- Pro → Enterprise: 10% (2/20 customers)

## Churn Prevention

### Why Customers Leave:
1. Not seeing ROI (efficiency not improving)
2. Too expensive (can't justify cost)
3. Switching to competitor (someone builds better product)
4. No longer running validator (shut down operations)

### Churn Prevention Tactics:

**1. Quarterly Business Reviews (PRO+ only)**
- Schedule 30-min call every 3 months
- Show exactly how much MEV they've improved
- Demonstrate ROI clearly
- Ask for feedback, add requested features

**2. Success Metrics Dashboard**
- Show "You've captured $X more MEV since subscribing"
- "Your efficiency improved Y% in 90 days"
- Make value undeniable

**3. Annual Discount**
- Offer 2 months free if they pay annually
- Locks them in for 12 months
- Improves cash flow

**4. Feature Roadmap Transparency**
- Show what's coming next
- Let customers vote on features
- Make them feel invested in the product

**Target Churn:** <5% monthly (industry standard is 5-7%)
